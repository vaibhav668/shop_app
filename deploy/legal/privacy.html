Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Bada Bazar ("we", "the shop") is a grocery shop in Thalisain, Uttarakhand. This policy explains what the Bada Bazar app and website collect, why, and what you can do about it. Contact: [shop email], [shop phone], [shop address], Thalisain, Pauri Garhwal, Uttarakhand.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics when you sign in: name, email address and Google account identifier. We never see your Google password.
  • Your mobile number, which you enter so the shop can call about a delivery.
  • Delivery addresses you save, including the name and phone for each.
  • Orders: what you bought, prices, payment method (cash on delivery), notes you add, and the order's progress.
  • Your cart and favourites.
  • A device notification token if you allow notifications, so we can tell you when your order moves along.
  • Basic technical logs (time, IP address, which page or action) kept for a short time to keep the service secure and working.

Recent searches are stored only on your phone and never sent to us. We do not use advertising trackers and do not sell your data.

Why we use it

  • To take, pack and deliver your orders and to contact you about them.
  • To show your order history, "Buy again" items and saved addresses.
  • To send order updates you have allowed.
  • To keep the shop's accounts and meet legal duties (for example tax records).
  • To protect the service from misuse.

Who else handles it

  • Google: sign-in.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): delivers notifications.
  • Cloudinary: stores product photos (no customer data).
  • Our hosting provider: runs the servers and database, in India where possible.

Shop staff see the name, phone, address and items of orders they are delivering. We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Your account data is kept while you have an account. Order records are kept for as long as accounting and tax law requires, even after you delete your account; they are then linked to "Deleted user" instead of you.

Your choices and rights

  • See and edit your name, number and addresses in the app under Account.
  • Turn notifications off in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Delete your account from the app (Account → Delete account) or by writing to us. See Delete your account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it by contacting the shop.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Access to customer data is limited to shop staff who need it. Passwords are never stored because sign-in is through Google.

Children

The app is meant for adults placing household grocery orders. It is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
