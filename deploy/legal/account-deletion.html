Delete your Bada Bazar account

Last updated: 12 October 2026

In the app (quickest)

  1. Open Bada Bazar and sign in.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

If an order is still on its way, deletion waits until it is delivered or cancelled.

Without the app

Email [shop email] from the Google account you use with Bada Bazar, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address, mobile number and Google sign-in link.
  • Your saved addresses, cart, favourites and notifications.
  • Your notification tokens; you are signed out everywhere.

What is kept, and for how long

Past order records (items, amounts, dates, and the delivery address used) are kept for as long as accounting and tax law requires, linked to "Deleted user" instead of you. Nothing else is kept.
