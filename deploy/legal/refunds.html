Cancellations and refunds

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Cancelling an order

  • You can cancel from the app (Orders → your order → Cancel order) until the shop confirms it.
  • After that, call the shop on [shop phone]; we will cancel if the order has not left for delivery.
  • The shop may cancel an order it cannot fulfil (for example an item ran out). You will see the reason in the app.

Payment and refunds

Orders are paid on delivery in cash or by UPI, so a cancelled order costs you nothing.

If something you paid for is missing, damaged, expired or not what you ordered, tell the delivery person at the door or call the shop within 24 hours of delivery. We will replace the item or refund its price, in cash or by UPI, within 7 days.

For hygiene reasons we cannot take back opened food items unless they were faulty.
